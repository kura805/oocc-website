About OOCC

Ogden Outdoor Climbing Club (OOCC) is a community-driven outdoor climbing group based in Ogden, Utah.

We welcome everyone — from complete beginners to experienced climbers looking for new challenges. Our goal is to build a strong climbing community, explore Utah climbing locations, and help each other grow through adventure and outdoor experiences.

Safety Notice

Outdoor climbing involves physical activity and inherent risk. All participants are responsible for their own safety, decisions, and awareness during climbs and events. OOCC encourages proper climbing practices, communication, preparation, and respect for the environment at all times.


For newer climbers or those without equipment, OOCC may provide limited shared gear during certain climbs and meetups when available. Participants are still encouraged to learn proper equipment usage and safety procedures before climbing.
